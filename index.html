What is Color Trading?

Color trading is a type of online trading game in which users predict the outcome of a color pattern. It is much like a guessing game where the trader chooses a color (such as red, green or blue), and if his prediction is correct, he makes a profit from it.

In this, people place bets based on either their own analysis or just by relying on luck, and the platform's algorithm decides which color will come next.

What is a Color Trading Prediction Chart?

A color trading prediction chart is a visual chart showing the color results of previous rounds. It helps players understand trends and patterns so that they can make better predictions for the next round.

This chart usually includes:

  • The latest color results (such as green, red, purple, etc.)
  • The probability of each color winning
  • Future predictions based on past trends

How Does Color Trading Work?

  • Choosing a platform – Users register on a colour trading website or app (most platforms are unregulated).
  • Betting on a colour – The game usually has three options:
    • Red
    • Green
    • Purple (sometimes it is also yellow or blue)
  • Betting with money – The user puts his money and chooses a colour.
  • Win or lose – If your chosen colour comes up in the results, you get a payout (usually up to 1.9x or 2x). If it doesn’t, you lose your money.
  • Manipulating the results – Many platforms are not transparent and may even manipulate the results, causing long-term losses to users.
